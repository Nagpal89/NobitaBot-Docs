**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

This page explains how to request help with NobitaBot (“the Bot”).

## 1. Official Support

For help, use the official NobitaBot or Orthus Esports Discord support channel:

Only trust links shared through your verified official channels.

## 2. What You Can Ask For Help With

You can request assistance with issues such as:

- Commands not responding or returning errors.
- Bot permissions, role hierarchy, or channel configuration.
- Moderation and staff-management workflows.
- Recruitment, registration, scrim, and tournament features.
- Incorrect behavior, suspected bugs, and feature suggestions.

Available support depends on the Bot’s current features and the support team’s availability.

## 3. How to Report a Bug

When reporting a bug, include:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. The command, button, menu, or feature involved.
3. What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
4. The approximate time of the issue and any relevant error message.
5. Screenshots or short reproduction steps, if useful.

Do not post bot tokens, API keys, passwords, database URLs, private credentials, or sensitive personal information in support channels. Redact these from screenshots and logs.

## 4. Server Configuration and Permissions

Some issues are caused by missing Discord permissions, role hierarchy, incorrect channel settings, or server-specific configuration. Server owners and administrators should verify these settings before reporting a problem.

## 5. Response Times

Support is provided as available. No fixed response or resolution time is guaranteed unless explicitly announced by the support team. Complex issues may require additional information or investigation.

## 6. Feature Requests

Feature suggestions are welcome, but submission does not guarantee that a feature will be implemented. Decisions may depend on feasibility, security, compatibility, and project priorities.

## 7. Respectful Communication

Be respectful to support staff and other community members. Spam, harassment, threats, or abuse may result in support restrictions in accordance with community rules.

## 8. Tournament and Moderation Disputes

NobitaBot may provide tools used by server staff and tournament organizers, but it does not automatically resolve every dispute. Contact the relevant server staff or event organizer for decisions about bans, registrations, team eligibility, results, or tournament rulings.

## 9. Privacy and Data Requests

For questions about information handled by the Bot, read the Privacy Policy and contact the verified privacy/support contact listed there. Do not post private data publicly.
