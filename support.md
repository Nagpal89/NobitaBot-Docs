# NobitaBot Support

Need help with NobitaBot, a command, a setup, or a bug?

## Official support server

Join the [NobitaBot Support Server](https://discord.gg/9JhMExCBAF).

## When reporting an issue

Please include:

- The command or feature you were using.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
- The full relevant error message or log, with tokens, passwords, database URLs, and other secrets removed.
- Your Discord server's relevant configuration details, without sharing private member information unnecessarily.

## Safety and privacy

Never share bot tokens, API keys, database credentials, passwords, or recovery codes in support channels. Support volunteers should not need your account password or bot token. If a secret is exposed, revoke or rotate it immediately.

Please be respectful and avoid posting private conversations, personal data, or screenshots containing information that is not needed to diagnose the issue.
